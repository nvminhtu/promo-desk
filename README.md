# Promo Desk — downloads

Mac app for people who make their own apps and post about them on Reddit.

It reads your product folder into a fact sheet, checks a draft against the rules of the subreddit you are
posting in, and writes in that sub's voice. It never posts, votes or sends DMs: you copy the text and press
Post yourself.

- Site: https://shotmatic.app
- macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon and Intel
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple

**Downloads are on the [Releases](../../releases) page.** This repository holds releases only; the source is private.
